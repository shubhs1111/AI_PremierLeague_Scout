Premier League 2026/27Scouting Lab

A data-driven recruitment model for every club in the 2026/27 Premier League. It measures how each team plays, rates every player per 90 minutes against others in the same role, finds where the squad falls short of the league, and ranks players who would close that gap within what the club has shown it can spend.

02Step 2 · How the current players are performing

Squad ratings by role

Rating 0–100: each player's per-90 numbers as percentiles against everyone whose main role is the same, weighted by what matters in that role, and pulled toward average when minutes are low. 2025/26 minutes count at 60%. Roles follow each player's listed position on Transfermarkt.

  • Every column follows the season picked below. "Both seasons" blends the two, with last season's minutes counting at 60%; this is the rating the gaps and targets use.
  • 2026/27 so far covers six gameweeks, so ratings there are pulled strongly toward average.
  • Match rating is an independent provider's average from your uploaded 2025/26 file, so it appears only under 2025/26. There is no provider rating for this season in the data.

03Step 3 · Where the squad falls short

Which positions need work

Each position is checked for six concrete problems: nobody playing there regularly, someone filling in out of position, a starter in the bottom third of the league for that position, a starter aged 31 or over (34 for goalkeepers, who peak later), too few above-average backups, and the position group ranking 15th or lower among the 20 clubs.

  • Critical the position is empty or covered out of position, plus at least one other problem.
  • High two or more problems, or the position is empty or covered.
  • Moderate one problem.   Low none.

A position is marked Sign when a new player would fix it: every Critical or High position, and a Moderate one only when the problem is the level of a starter. Age or thin cover on its own is a plan for later, not a reason to buy now, so those positions are marked Hold and get no targets.

Starters are the players with the most minutes this season. The number of starters per position follows how the club has lined up (back three or four, two or three central midfielders, one or two strikers), and the holding midfielder is whichever regular central midfielder plays the most defensive game. Each player counts in one position only. Percentiles compare a player with the league's regulars in that position (450+ minutes) over both seasons. "Nth strongest of 20" compares the club's starters in that position with the starters at every other club.

04Step 4 · Who would take the squad to the next level

Ranked targets for every position marked Sign

Candidates must out-rate the weakest current starter in the role by at least 3 points, rank in at least the 60th percentile for the role, have 900+ league minutes over the two seasons and minutes this season, and be affordable: Transfermarkt market value no higher than the average of the club's three most valuable players plus 25% (FPL price is the fallback when a player has no value). A contract with 18 months or less left makes a sale more likely. Clubs rarely sign regulars from clearly stronger clubs, so those are scored down or removed. One player can only join one club, so shortlists are drawn up like a draft: each round, every position marked Sign takes its best remaining candidate, the most urgent needs first, and no player appears on more than two shortlists. The number of positions differs from club to club.

Limits

  • The six 2026/27 gameweeks are a small sample, which is why last season is blended in.
  • Ages, market values and contracts come from the Transfermarkt dataset you uploaded, which was last refreshed during 2025/26, so values lag this season's form. About 93% of current Premier League players link by name; the rest (mostly newly promoted squads) fall back to FPL price and a neutral age score.
  • Candidates come only from Premier League squads. Players abroad need a comparable per-match dataset.
  • Each player is offered to at most two clubs, the most urgent needs first, so a club further down the draft can be left with weaker options or none.
  • Roles follow each player's listed Transfermarkt position. The few players without one get a role from k-means clustering on playing style within their FPL position.
  • Reports are written from the same facts shown on each card. Stats in the reports and comparison bars are plain per-90 numbers over 2025/26 and 2026/27 combined; the rating itself weights last season at 60% and adjusts defensive actions for possession.

Data: FPL Core Insights (match and player-match stats, 2025/26 and 2026/27), plus uploaded 2025/26 season stats, 2000–2025 results and Transfermarkt player profiles.
